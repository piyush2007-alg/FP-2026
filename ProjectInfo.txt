<---Field Visit Report – Poultry Farm Management System--->

As part of our academic project on the Poultry Farm Management System, our team conducted a field visit to a local poultry farm to understand its daily operations. 
During the visit, we interacted with the farm owner and asked questions about poultry management, feeding practices, vaccination, egg production, and the challenges 
faced in running the farm.The owner explained the different breeds of hens, including Kadaknath, Desi, and Sonalika. We learned that Kadaknath eggs are highly 
nutritious and contain more than 120 types of vitamins and nutrients, making them popular for their health benefits. The Desi breed produces around 15–20 eggs per
month, while Sonalika hens can produce up to 365 eggs per year under proper care. The farm maintains 250–300 birds and keeps records of egg production and vaccination.
We also learned about the Lasota and Gumboro vaccines, which are administered according to the recommended vaccination schedule to protect the birds from diseases.
The birds are mainly fed a balanced diet consisting of wheat, maize (corn), and bajra (pearl millet) to ensure healthy growth and better egg production.The owner also 
discussed common challenges such as eye infections(bitwin) and disease management. This field visit provided us with valuable practical knowledge about poultry farming 
and highlighted the importance of proper nutrition, vaccination, hygiene, and record management in maintaining a successful poultry farm.
